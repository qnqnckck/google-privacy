Support

Shape Escape: Puzzle Rescue is an offline-first spatial logic puzzle. No account or login is required.

How to play

Select a shape to display every valid movement arrow. Tap an indicated destination cell to move. Free all required shapes to complete the level.

Hearts, undo, and restart

A failed attempt or restart may consume hearts. Hearts recover over time. A rewarded ad can refill hearts when an ad is available. Undo lets you reverse a move.

Unlimited Play

The one-time Unlimited Play purchase removes heart limits and rewarded ads and unlocks access across all 200 levels. Use Restore Purchases on the same store account if your entitlement is missing.

Troubleshooting

Restart the app, confirm your device is online for purchases or ads, and update to the latest version. Puzzle progress remains on the device. Reinstalling the app may remove local progress.
